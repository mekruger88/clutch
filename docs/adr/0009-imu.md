# ADR-0009: GY-521 (MPU-6050) as the v1 IMU

- Status: Accepted 2026-08-26

## Context
Mecanum dead reckoning is slip-limited by design, so ADR-0008 made an IMU a requirement rather than an enhancement. The GY-521 carries an MPU-6050: three-axis gyro plus three-axis accelerometer. **Six axes, no magnetometer.**

## Options
1. GY-521, already owned - $0, yaw rate but no absolute heading.
2. BNO085-class part with on-chip fusion - about $25, includes a magnetometer, which is often useless near four motors and eight servos indoors anyway.
3. No IMU, wheel odometry only - rejected; strafe and rotation drift are unacceptable.

## Decision
Option 1. Read by the Teensy on a dedicated I2C bus, powered at 3.3 V, published as sensor_msgs/Imu through micro-ROS at a fixed rate.

## Consequences
This provides yaw **rate**, plus bounded roll and pitch from gravity. It does **not** provide absolute heading - integrated gyro yaw drifts without bound, so SLAM owns heading via the map->odom correction.

Fusion: robot_localization, two_d_mode, wheel vx/vy plus IMU vyaw only. IMU orientation and linear acceleration are not fused. The differential-yaw configuration is the alternative to test against the same recorded bag.

Initial zero-rate output tolerance is +/-20 deg/s, with another +/-20 deg/s across temperature, so boot-time stationary bias zeroing is mandatory and must be repeated every boot. Mount rigidly near the rotation center, away from motor wiring, with the orientation documented and the imu->base_link static transform matching reality.

## Verification
IMU-001 WHO_AM_I correct and SDA/SCL idle at or below 3.3 V **before connecting to the Teensy**. IMU-002 drift under 2 deg/min after zeroing. IMU-003 360 deg rotation within 2 percent over ten trials. IMU-004 bias shift under 1 deg/s with all four motors running. IMU-005 EKF on versus off, measured.

## Reversal trigger
IMU-004 shows vibration corrupting the rate signal beyond mitigation, or drift between SLAM corrections proves too large. Then move to an on-chip-fusion part.
