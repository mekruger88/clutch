# 2026-08-26 / 27 - Clean-slate restart, ten decisions locked

**Goal.** Restart the project from a blank sheet and lock only the decisions that block the bare-bones baseline.

**Did.** Made D1-D10 in dependency order, most-constraining first. Battery replaced on spec rather than measurement alone. Drive stage moved to paralleled TB6612 channels. Compute topology kept on owned hardware with a staged Jetson-plus-MCU bring-up. ROS pinned to Humble because the Nano cannot leave JetPack 4. Low-level controller changed from Mega to Teensy 4.1. Kinematics split by timescale. IMU accepted with its limits stated. Hardware e-stop specified to the ISO 13850 latching pattern.

**Broke / caught.** Three inventory and spec errors found before any money moved: the component catalog reused ASINs across unrelated items; MG996R torque and current figures in the catalog disagreed with the manufacturer page; and nothing in the inventory gives the Jetson any wireless capability, which blocks untethered operation until an M.2 card is added.

**Blocking next.** CTL-000: measure encoder A-channel output voltage before ordering a Teensy. Teensy 4.x GPIO is not 5 V tolerant and 5 V on a pin destroys the board. This one measurement decides whether the Teensy path costs $35 or $35 plus a level-shifter design on eight lines.

**Next.** Phase 1: kinematics library with randomized IK/FK round-trip tests, placeholder URDF with real chassis dimensions, CI green, ADR pages written.

**AI acceleration.** Used an assistant as an adversarial design reviewer working one decision at a time in dependency order, with every recommendation required to carry a quantified trade-off and a falsifiable bench test. Value came from foreclosure analysis rather than answers: that the Nano's JetPack ceiling locks the ROS distro for every machine including the ground station, that the TB6612's supply limit constrains battery chemistry, and that keeping the Mega forecloses micro-ROS entirely. It also reversed its own recommendation mid-session when the Mega's interrupt budget was checked against the actual pin map. Every call was mine.
